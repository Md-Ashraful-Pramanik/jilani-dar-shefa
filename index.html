01 / About

A practitioner who starts by listening.

❝

Every consultation begins with the whole person — not only the symptom that brought them in.

Dr. Salma Afroz offers individual consultations in a respectful and unhurried setting.

Her approach makes room for the details of each person’s experience—physical concerns, daily routines, and general wellbeing—while encouraging open communication with all members of your healthcare team.

02 / The approach

What to expect

A consultation is a conversation. You will have time to describe what brought you in, ask questions, and understand the next steps.

03 / Areas of interest

Support shaped around the individual

People seek complementary care for many different reasons. These are areas of particular practice interest, not promises of treatment or cure.

04 / Before your visit

A little preparation helps us listen well

You do not need perfect answers. A few notes about your concerns and health history simply help make the conversation more useful.
